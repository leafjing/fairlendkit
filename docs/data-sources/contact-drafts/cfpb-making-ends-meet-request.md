## Message draft

Hello Consumer Financial Protection Bureau team,

We are documenting a reproducible, privacy-bounded research use of the Making
Ends Meet Survey. The CFPB Public Data Inventory still identifies the series,
and the public-use-file user guide remains available, but the inventory's
program link and the previously indexed public-data path currently redirect to
the general data and research page. We have not downloaded, reconstructed, or
redistributed a data package.

Could you please clarify the following?

1. What is the current official landing or download page for the Making Ends
   Meet public-use files?
2. Which exact samples or waves are currently published, and what publisher-
   issued release or revision identity should be used for each one?
3. Is there an official package member manifest, version history, stable URL,
   or publisher checksum? Can package bytes change at the same URL?
4. Which user agreement, license, attribution, automated-analysis, and
   redistribution terms apply to the public-use files and documentation?
5. Where are the authoritative questionnaires, codebooks, weighting and
   variance instructions, missing-value definitions, and revision notes?
6. For credit-derived fields, what are the survey reference dates, credit-
   record observation dates, outcome/performance windows, maturity rules, and
   censoring conventions for each sample?
7. Are there additional privacy, linkage, re-identification, disclosure, or
   publication restrictions beyond those stated in the public-use materials?

We intend to use survey hardship and coverage measures only for data-quality,
representation, and fairness analysis unless CFPB documentation supports a
separate, temporally mature credit outcome. We will not reinterpret survey
difficulty as loan default or convert survey responses into transaction events.

Thank you.
